How Derma-Scan Works Step-by-Step

Understanding our two-stage AI screening pipeline in plain language, along with why human dermatologists remain irreplaceable.

Step 1 • Local Pre-Check

Photo Selection & Consent

You select a JPEG, PNG, or WebP image (up to 10 MB) or capture a photo with your camera. Your browser verifies the file format and asks for your explicit screening consent.

Step 2 • Feature Extraction

Foundation Model Embeddings

Your image is sent via a multipart POST request (field file) to our FastAPI service, which encodes the image and extracts a high-dimensional feature embedding using the Google Derm Foundation SavedModel.

Step 3 • Classifier Prediction

Keras Neural Classifier Inference

The extracted embedding vector is passed into our custom trained Keras classifier (derm_classifier_model.h5) to compute class probabilities and identify the top matching class index and confidence percentage.

Step 4 • Educational Context

Guided Next Steps

Derma-Scan displays the output alongside plain-language condition guides and reminders on when to schedule an in-person dermatologist evaluation.

Important Limitations to Keep in Mind

While machine learning models can recognize visual similarities across training datasets, they have strict boundaries:

  • Lighting & Angles Matter: Shadows, camera blur, or warm indoor bulbs can shift model predictions significantly.
  • No Physical Exam: An algorithm cannot feel whether a bump is firm, warm, tender, or scaly beneath the surface.
  • No Medical History: The model does not know how long a spot has been present, whether it itches, or your family history.
  • Not a Diagnostic Clearance: Never use a screening result to delay seeing a doctor for a changing or concerning mole.
