How Derma-Scan Works

A friendly, transparent workflow designed to help you organize visual observations before speaking with a clinician.

Step 01

Capture or Upload

Select a clear, well-lit photo of the skin area in JPEG, PNG, or WebP format—or capture one directly using your device camera.

Step 02

AI Screening

With your consent, your image is sent to our FastAPI screening service, which extracts visual embeddings and runs our classifier model.

Trust & Medical Safety First

What Derma-Scan Can—and Cannot—Do

Computer vision models can highlight visual patterns that resemble training examples, making them a helpful educational starting point. However, a photograph cannot feel skin texture, measure depth, take your medical history, or perform a dermoscopic biopsy.

  • Not a Medical Diagnosis: Output is generated by a machine learning model for informational screening only.
  • Cannot Rule Out Serious Conditions: High or low confidence scores never rule out melanoma, basal cell carcinoma, squamous cell carcinoma, or infections.
  • Always Consult a Specialist: Seek prompt in-person medical evaluation for any changing, bleeding, painful, or persistent lesion.
