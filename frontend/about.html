Our Story & Ethics

Making Skin Literacy Calm, Friendly & Honest

We built Derma.co and the Derma-Scan feature to bridge the gap between overwhelming internet searches and thoughtful dermatology visits.

Our Mission

Searching for skin symptoms online often leads to alarming photos or confusing jargon. Derma.co combines a warm, approachable design system with an AI-assisted screening workflow so teenagers and adults can explore educational skin information calmly.

Core Project Values

  • Safety Over Hype: We never claim clinical certification or diagnostic certainty.
  • Inclusive Education: Our 37 condition guides use clear language relevant across diverse skin tones.
  • Respectful Privacy: Images are only submitted when the user ticks the explicit consent box.
