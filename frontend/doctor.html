Appointment Prep

How to Prepare for Your Dermatology Visit

Bringing clear observations helps your dermatologist make the most of your appointment:

  • Timeline: Note when the spot or rash first appeared and whether it has changed in size, shape, or color.
  • Symptoms: Mention if the area itches, burns, bleeds, crusts, or feels tender.
  • Current Products: Bring a list or photos of cleansers, sunscreens, cosmetics, and any topical creams you use.
  • Clean Skin: Arrive without makeup or nail polish if having a full-body or facial skin check.
